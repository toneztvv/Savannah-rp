Your First Hour

GETTING ESTABLISHED

1. Keep it small

Don't try to run the city on day one. Grab a legal job, drive around, talk to people, and let your character find their feet. The best stories start slow.

2. Find work

There's honest money everywhere — delivery and freight gigs, mechanic work, taxi/nightlife jobs, and shops that hire. Use it to buy a car, rent a room, and get a footing before anything ambitious.

3. Housing

Rent a room or buy an apartment/house through real estate. A home gives you storage, a place to lie low, and a base for your roleplay.

4. Legal vs. underground

Both paths matter equally here. You can build a business and a badge, or a crew and a corner — or drift between them. Just make it believable: reputations are earned over time, not claimed.

5. Roleplay tips

  • Talk to people. Downtime conversations build the city more than shootouts do.
  • Play to lose sometimes. A scene where your character gets robbed, arrested, or humbled is still a great scene.
  • Never break character to argue. Take issues to a ticket after.
  • If something's confusing, ask in Discord — nobody expects you to know everything.

6. Get help

Stuck, bugged, or unsure? Open a ticket in Discord and staff will sort it out. See the Support page for how tickets work.
