SAVANNAH RP COMMUNITY STANDARDS

RULES & GUIDELINES

These rules are the current Savannah RP foundation. Staff may update details, limits, zones, timers, gang caps and other specifics as the server develops.

ZERO TOLERANCE: Hacking, menus, cheats, injected software, exploits, duplication, unauthorized modifications or knowingly abusing a bug can result in a permanent ban.

RULE CATEGORIES

Core Conduct Cheating & Exploits Roleplay Standards Combat & Conflict Gangs & Crews Crime & Robberies PD / EMS Zones & Scenes Characters Voice / OOC Economy & Scams Staff & Reports
01

CORE CONDUCT

Respect Everyone

No harassment, hate speech, targeted bullying, threats, doxxing, sexual harassment or discriminatory behavior.

Use Common Sense

If something clearly damages fair RP or is being used to gain an unfair advantage, staff may act even if it is not written word-for-word here.

Value Roleplay

Do not intentionally ruin scenes, troll active RP, constantly break character or treat every situation like a deathmatch.

No Ban Evasion

Using alternate accounts, identifiers or another person’s account to avoid punishment is prohibited.

02

HACKING, EXPLOITS & UNFAIR ADVANTAGES

No hacking or mod menus. This includes aimbot, ESP, wallhacks, trigger bots, freecam abuse, god mode, teleporting, injected menus or similar cheats.

No exploiting. Do not abuse bugs, glitches, animation exploits, inventory exploits, duplication methods, vehicle exploits, economy bugs or broken scripts.

No bug abuse. If you find a serious bug, report it. Repeating it for money, items, combat advantage or progression can be punished as exploiting.

No unauthorized software or modifications. Anything that gives information or an advantage normal players do not have is prohibited.

No real-money trading outside approved systems. Do not buy, sell or trade server money/items/accounts through unauthorized methods.

03

ROLEPLAY STANDARDS

Metagaming

Do not use information your character did not learn in-game. Streams, Discord, clips, another character, DMs or spectator information cannot be used IC.

Powergaming

Do not force unrealistic actions or outcomes on another player, abuse mechanics to guarantee a result, or perform actions your character could not reasonably do.

FailRP

Act in a believable way for the situation. Extremely unrealistic behavior, ignoring injuries, ignoring consequences or intentionally breaking immersion can be FailRP.

FearRP / Value of Life

Your character should value their life. If realistically outnumbered, restrained, held at gunpoint or seriously threatened, react appropriately to the danger.

New Life Rule

After a completed death/respawn, do not return to the same active scene for revenge or immediately continue the conflict. Exact timers may be updated later.

Character Separation

Knowledge, money, relationships and grudges from one character cannot automatically transfer to another character.

04

COMBAT & CONFLICT

RDM — Random Deathmatch: Do not attack or kill players without valid roleplay reason and appropriate interaction/scene context.

VDM — Vehicle Deathmatch: Do not intentionally use a vehicle as a weapon without a valid and believable RP reason.

Combat Logging: Do not disconnect, force-crash, switch characters or hide from the server to avoid an active scene, arrest, robbery or consequences.

Combat Storing: Do not stash weapons/items/vehicles while actively being pursued or while a conflict is still ongoing simply to prevent loss.

Revenge RP: Do not immediately hunt players after being downed/killed when your character would not reasonably retain the information.

Third Partying: Do not jump into conflicts you have no RP involvement in solely to get kills, loot or advantage.

05

GANGS, CREWS & ORGANIZED GROUPS

Gang Identity

Groups should have a clear identity, story and roleplay purpose. Existing solely to farm fights is not the goal.

Gang Limits

Member caps, conflict caps and active-scene limits may be set by staff. These numbers are intentionally left flexible for the server launch period.

Alliances

Do not create oversized unofficial alliances to bypass gang limits or overwhelm another group. Temporary RP cooperation should still make sense.

Gang Wars

Wars need valid escalation and should remain roleplay-driven. Endless KOS behavior, spawn camping or repeated harassment is prohibited.

Territory

Territory claims must be roleplayed. Owning a neighborhood does not automatically make every outsider KOS.

Leaving a Gang

Characters leaving a criminal organization should handle consequences through RP. Rules around memory wipes or cooldowns may be added later.

06

CRIME, ROBBERIES & HOSTAGES

Criminal scenes must have RP behind them. Do not repeatedly rob the same player purely to grief them.

Hostages should be actual participants in the scene. Do not use obvious friends/fake hostages solely to manipulate robbery mechanics.

Do not force players to empty entire bank accounts, transfer property or surrender assets beyond what server rules/mechanics allow.

Robbery cooldowns, hostage limits, police requirements and weapon restrictions may be added once the final economy and scripts are locked in.

Kidnapping, torture and serious violent RP must remain within platform/server rules and should never be used to harass another player.

07

PD, EMS & GOVERNMENT RP

Respect Emergency RP

Do not constantly bait police or EMS simply to start chases, shootings or disrupt their roleplay.

Cop Baiting

Repeatedly committing pointless actions directly in front of PD solely to force attention or a pursuit is prohibited.

EMS Scenes

When EMS is treating a scene, give them a reasonable chance to perform medical RP. Do not use EMS as a reset button to immediately rejoin combat.

Corruption

PD/EMS/government corruption is only allowed if server leadership explicitly approves it. Do not assume corruption is automatically permitted.

08

SAFE ZONES, GREEN ZONES & ACTIVE SCENES

Any future safe/green zones will be listed by staff. Do not use a protected area to escape an active scene or chase.

Do not camp hospitals, spawn locations, garages or similar areas to repeatedly target players.

Do not interfere with staff scenes, player reports or administrative actions.

Specific zone maps and restrictions will be added once Savannah’s final MLOs and city locations are confirmed.

09

CHARACTERS & IDENTITY

Use reasonable character names. Names created purely to troll or impersonate public figures/staff may be rejected.

Do not impersonate staff, law enforcement or another player outside legitimate in-character RP.

Characters should have believable motivations and continuity. Constantly changing identity to avoid consequences is prohibited.

Do not transfer information or assets between alternate characters to bypass consequences or progression.

10

VOICE, OOC & COMMUNICATION

Stay In Character

Keep OOC discussion out of active RP whenever possible. Use the proper support/report channels for disputes.

OOC Abuse

Do not use OOC chat/voice to insult, threaten, argue with or harass another player over an in-character situation.

External Communication

Discord calls, streams, chats and DMs cannot be used to coordinate IC actions unless the server explicitly allows that communication method.

Soundboards

Soundboards/music must not be spammed or used to disrupt RP. Staff may require you to stop if it interferes with scenes.

11

ECONOMY, TRADES & SCAMS

Paid Custom Chains: Custom chains purchased through the approved Savannah RP store are cosmetic-only protected entitlements. They cannot be stolen, dropped, transferred, sold, or permanently lost through normal roleplay.

Do not exploit scripts or mechanics to duplicate money, items, vehicles, weapons or property.

Scamming rules will depend on Savannah’s final economy. Until clarified, scams should remain reasonable, roleplayed and not abuse UI/mechanics.

Do not intentionally misrepresent Tebex purchases, staff decisions or out-of-character transactions as in-character obligations.

Account selling, currency selling and unauthorized real-money exchanges are prohibited.

12

STAFF, REPORTS & ENFORCEMENT

Follow staff instructions during an administrative situation. If you disagree, use the appeal/report process afterward.

Do not lie in reports, intentionally hide evidence or submit edited clips meant to mislead staff.

Do not spam reports because staff has not responded immediately.

Staff may use context, severity, prior history and intent when determining punishment.

Rules can be updated as Savannah RP develops. Major updates should be announced through official community channels.

CURRENT DRAFT

MORE SPECIFICS WILL BE ADDED.

Gang caps, robbery cooldowns, safe zones, scene limits, weapon restrictions, police requirements, business rules and other server-specific numbers can be filled in once Savannah’s final systems are ready.
