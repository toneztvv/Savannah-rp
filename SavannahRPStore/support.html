Help & Tickets

SAVANNAH SUPPORT

All support runs through tickets in our Discord. Open the right one, include what's asked for, and staff will handle it. Don't confront staff mid-scene — take it to a ticket.

Ticket Types

WHICH TICKET DO I OPEN?

Help

General Support

Stuck, bugged, lost a vehicle, question about the server. Include your character name and what happened.

Report a Bug

Bug Report

Something broken or exploitable. Steps to reproduce it, plus a clip or screenshot. Don't abuse it in the meantime.

Report a Player

Player Report

Rule-breaking you witnessed. Their name/description, when it happened, and clip or screenshot evidence — reports without evidence usually can't be actioned.

Appeal

Ban / Punishment Appeal

Your Discord ID, the punishment, and an honest account of what happened. Dishonesty ends the appeal.

Store

Purchase Support

A purchase didn't deliver or delivered wrong. Include your Tebex transaction / order ID.

Custom Orders

Custom Chain / Clothing / Vehicle

After buying, open this ticket with your Tebex invoice (required before work starts) and your reference image.

Custom purchases — invoice required

For any custom cosmetic order, staff must verify your Tebex invoice/receipt in the ticket before any work begins. Staff will never ask for your password, full card number, CVV, or banking login — if someone does, it's not us.

FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

How do I join?

Join the Discord, verify in the welcome channel, read the rules, then connect. Full walkthrough on the New Players page.

Is there a whitelist to play?

Not to play as a civilian. Departments (PD, EMS), staff, and some organizations have applications — see the Apply page.

Is the store pay-to-win?

No. It's cosmetic and convenience only — no money, weapons, stats, or gameplay advantages. Details on the Store page.

My custom chain/clothing came back wrong.

Keep your original ticket open. Describe what's off and support will keep working it until it's right — no need to buy again.

Can I get a refund if I'm banned?

No. A ban doesn't entitle you to a refund, and a purchase never exempts you from the rules. See the Refund Policy.

I was banned unfairly.

Open a ban appeal ticket with your side of it and any evidence. Staff review appeals — arguing in other channels won't help.
