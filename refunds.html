REFUND POLICY

How Savannah RP handles purchase and delivery issues.

1. Purchase Problems

If a paid package was not delivered, was delivered incorrectly, or has a technical issue, contact Savannah RP support with the Tebex transaction ID and relevant account or character information.

2. Digital Deliverables

Many Savannah RP products are digital or cosmetic services. Once work has started or a digital entitlement has been delivered, refund eligibility may be limited, subject to applicable consumer law and the payment platform's rules.

3. Custom Work

For custom chains, artwork, branding or other personalized work, pricing covers the defined deliverables in the package description. If custom work has already begun, cancellation or refund eligibility may depend on how much work has been completed.

4. Bans and Rule Enforcement

A suspension, ban, character wipe or other enforcement action does not automatically entitle a customer to a refund. Store purchases do not exempt a player from Savannah RP rules.

5. Chargebacks

Do not use a chargeback as a substitute for contacting support about a legitimate delivery problem. Fraudulent or abusive payment disputes may result in account restrictions and may be reported through the payment platform's normal process.

6. Mandatory Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy removes any refund, cancellation, or consumer rights that cannot legally be waived in your jurisdiction.
