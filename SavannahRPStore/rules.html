SAVANNAH RULES

Enough structure for roleplay to matter, without turning every scene into a rulebook. Breaking these risks a warning, kick, or ban depending on severity and history. Staff decisions are final; appeals go through a ticket.

The short version

Play your character honestly, value your life, don't ruin other people's roleplay, and keep out-of-character drama out of the city. Not sure something's allowed? Ask in a ticket first.

1   Core Conduct
  • No cheating. Mod menus, injectors, automation macros, texture/model edits for advantage, or any third-party tool that alters the game is an instant permanent ban.
  • No exploiting. Abusing bugs, duplication, map holes, animation glitches or unintended mechanics. Found a bug? Report it — don't use it.
  • No real-money trading of in-game items, money, or accounts outside the official store.
  • No impersonating staff or claiming authority you don't have.
2   Roleplay Standards
  • Stay in character. Use in-city voice/text for in-city talk. Keep OOC chatter to Discord or clearly marked OOC.
  • No metagaming. Don't use information your character didn't learn in the city — streams, Discord, OOC messages — to act in RP.
  • No powergaming. Don't force outcomes, roleplay unrealistic abilities, or deny others a fair chance to react.
  • Value of Life. Treat your character's life as real. Comply when a gun is on you and resisting would realistically get you killed.
  • Fear RP. Show fear when it makes sense — a weapon pointed at you, being outnumbered, serious injury.
  • New Life Rule. A character who is downed and respawns at a hospital forgets the events leading to their death and does not return to the scene.
  • No FailRP. No non-realistic driving, no random unprovoked chaos, no breaking immersion for a laugh.
3   Combat
  • No RDM. No killing without proper roleplay and a real in-character reason.
  • No VDM. Vehicles are not weapons unless roleplay clearly leads there.
  • Initiation required. Give a clear demand and a chance to comply before opening fire, unless you're actively being shot at.
  • No combat logging. Disconnecting to escape RP, a fight, or a ban is treated as the worst outcome for your character.
  • Safe zones. No crime, weapons drawn, or combat at hospitals, police stations, or other marked safe areas.
  • No spawn camping or waiting outside jail/hospital to attack players leaving.
4   Crime, Gangs & Robberies
  • Crews and organizations should be registered with staff and have grounded backstories.
  • Robberies and hostages need real roleplay, a reason, and an escape plan — not just "hands up, give me everything."
  • Player counts, cooldowns, hostage limits and territory rules for major crime are set by staff and posted in Discord. Follow the current numbers.
  • No hostage-farming — repeatedly taking the same person or holding hostages with no intent to RP it out.
  • Gang conflict should build over time. No same-day "war declared, everyone dies" escalation.
5   Police & EMS
  • On-duty PD and EMS represent the city. Corruption RP is limited and must be approved by department leadership.
  • No cop baiting — provoking police with no roleplay reason just to start a chase or shootout.
  • EMS is neutral. Do not attack medics or prevent them from working a scene.
  • PD should give suspects a realistic chance to surrender and follow use-of-force expectations.
6   Community
  • No harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats, doxxing, or targeting players in or out of character.
  • Keep NSFW and heavy real-world topics out of the city and the Discord.
  • No advertising other servers, communities, or services without permission.
  • Streaming and clips are welcome — but stream sniping and using stream info in RP is metagaming.
  • One character personality per slot. Don't blur characters or use alts to dodge consequences.
7   Economy & Store
  • The store is cosmetic and convenience only. Nothing you buy gives money, weapons, stats, or any gameplay advantage.
  • No scamming outside of a believable, roleplayed situation — and never for real money.
  • Chargebacks for delivered purchases result in a permanent ban. Real delivery problem? Open a support ticket.
8   Staff, Reports & Appeals
  • Report rule-breaking through a ticket with clip/screenshot evidence — never confront staff or argue mid-scene.
  • Ban evasion (alt accounts, new Discords) extends the ban and can make it permanent.
  • Appeal a punishment through the appeal ticket. Be honest — dishonesty in an appeal ends it.
  • Staff may adjust or clarify these rules at any time. Major changes are announced in Discord.

Specific numbers — gang caps, robbery cooldowns, hostage limits, PD requirements, weapon limits — are maintained by staff and posted in the Discord information channels.
