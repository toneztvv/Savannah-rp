SAVANNAH RP SUPPORT

TICKET CENTER

Open the right ticket, include the right information, and keep everything in one place so support can help you faster.

01

CUSTOM CHAIN / PURCHASE

Use this after buying a custom chain.

  • Tebex invoice or receipt is required.
  • Include transaction/order ID if shown.
  • Staff may ask for the checkout email to match the order.
  • After verification, upload the exact chain image in the same ticket.
  • Keep the ticket open until the chain is completed and any problems are resolved.
02

STORE / DELIVERY HELP

Use this if a paid item has not been delivered or your order is not showing correctly.

  • Include your invoice/receipt.
  • Include your transaction/order ID.
  • Explain what you purchased and what is missing.
03

BUG REPORT

Use this for scripts, menus, inventory, housing, vehicles, jobs, UI, or other server problems.

  • Explain exactly what happened.
  • Include steps to reproduce it if possible.
  • Add screenshots or clips when useful.
  • Do not abuse a bug while waiting for support.
04

PLAYER REPORT

Use this for rule violations that need staff review.

  • Include player name/ID if known.
  • Explain what happened clearly.
  • Attach relevant clips or screenshots.
  • Do not argue with the reported player inside the ticket.
05

BAN / PUNISHMENT APPEAL

Use this to request a review of a punishment.

  • Be truthful about what happened.
  • Explain why you believe the decision should be reviewed.
  • Do not spam multiple tickets for the same appeal.
06

GENERAL SUPPORT

Use this when none of the other ticket categories fit.

  • Describe what you need help with.
  • Include your Discord username and character name when relevant.
  • Keep all replies about the same issue in one ticket.

PAYMENT SAFETY

NEVER SEND SENSITIVE PAYMENT INFORMATION.

Savannah RP support may need your Tebex invoice/receipt, transaction or order ID, and possibly the email used at checkout to verify an order. Staff should never ask for your password, full card number, CVV, banking password, or online banking login.

ONE ISSUE. ONE TICKET.

KEEP THE TICKET OPEN UNTIL IT'S RESOLVED.

If support is already helping you, continue in the same ticket instead of creating duplicates. This keeps the full history together and makes it easier to verify what has already been done.
