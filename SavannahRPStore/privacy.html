PRIVACY POLICY

A starter privacy notice for the Savannah RP website and community.

1. Information We May Receive

Depending on how Savannah RP is configured, we may receive information such as your Discord identity, platform/server identifiers, character information, support messages, moderation records, transaction references, and technical logs required to operate the server.

2. Store and Payment Data

Payments are handled by the store/payment provider used by Savannah RP. Savannah RP does not collect or store full payment card details through this website.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide server access, deliver purchases, verify entitlements, operate support, enforce rules, prevent fraud or abuse, investigate technical issues, and improve Savannah RP.

4. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers used to operate Savannah RP, when required to investigate abuse or fraud, when required by law, or when otherwise necessary to protect the community and services.

5. Retention

Records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose they were collected, including support, moderation, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect account, server and support information. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or other treatment of personal data. Contact Savannah RP support to make a privacy request.
