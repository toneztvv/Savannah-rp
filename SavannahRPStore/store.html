SAVANNAH STORE

Support the city without buying power. When it opens it runs through Tebex and stays strictly cosmetic and convenience — nothing you buy gives money, weapons, stats, or an edge.

No pay-to-win. Full stop.

Savannah RP follows Rockstar and Tebex monetization rules. Purchases only ever cover cosmetics, personalization, and small convenience — and every product states exactly what you get.

What the store will offer

Supporter Tiers

A Discord role, name styling, a supporter badge, supporter-only channels. Recognition, not power.

Queue Priority

Skip some of the wait to join. No in-city money, items, or advantage of any kind.

Custom Clothing & Style

Original outfits, accessories and character-style cosmetics. Made to order from your reference.

Custom Chains & Jewelry

One custom cosmetic chain from your artwork. Protected — can't be stolen, dropped, or lost.

Vehicle Cosmetics

Approved liveries, wraps and vanity plates. No speed, handling, armor or durability changes.

Home & Business Branding

Interior themes, decor, signage and uniform cosmetics for properties and approved businesses.

What we will never sell

  • In-game money or currency
  • Weapons, ammo, armor or combat items
  • Stat boosts or gameplay advantages
  • Paid progression or XP
  • Loot boxes, crates or any chance mechanic
  • Staff powers or in-character authority
  • Anything that beats another player for cash

Custom chain policy

Paid custom chains are a protected cosmetic entitlement — they can't be stolen, dropped, traded, transferred, or permanently lost through death, robbery or normal roleplay. By submitting artwork you confirm you own it or have permission to use it. Savannah RP may reject designs containing copyrighted, trademarked, or third-party material.
