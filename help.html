FAQ

QUICK ANSWERS.

Is Savannah RP pay-to-win?

No. Store focus is cosmetics and recognition, not combat or economy power.

Can I buy money or weapons?

No. Those are not sold.

How do custom chains work?

Pay first on Tebex, open a Custom Chain ticket with invoice proof, then upload your image. Staff never need passwords or full card numbers.

Where do I apply for PD / EMS / business?

Use the Apply category.

How do I join?

Start in Play for Discord, connect and the new player guide.

TICKETS

PICK THE RIGHT TYPE.

01

CUSTOM CHAIN / PURCHASE

After buying a custom chain. Include Tebex invoice/receipt. Upload the image in the same ticket after verification.

02

STORE / DELIVERY

Missing delivery or order issues. Include invoice and order ID.

03

BUG REPORT

Scripts, menus, housing, vehicles, jobs or UI problems. Include steps and screenshots.

04

PLAYER REPORT

Rule breaks. Include names/IDs and evidence. Do not argue in the ticket.

05

APPEAL

Ban or punishment review. Be honest; do not spam duplicates.

06

GENERAL

Anything else. One issue, one ticket until resolved.

Staff may need invoice, order ID, or checkout email. Never passwords, full card numbers, CVV, or bank logins.
