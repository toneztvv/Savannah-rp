Requirements

You must be 18+, have a working microphone, own a legitimate copy of the base game, and have read the rules. That's it — no interview to play as a civilian.

Your first hour

Keep it small

Don't try to run the city on day one. Grab a legal job, drive around, talk to people, and let your character find their feet. The best stories start slow.

Find work

There's honest money everywhere — delivery and freight gigs, mechanic work, taxi and nightlife jobs, shops that hire. Use it to buy a car, rent a room, and get a footing.

Housing

Rent a room or buy an apartment or house through real estate. A home gives you storage, a place to lie low, and a base for your roleplay.

Legal vs. underground

Both paths matter equally here. Build a business and a badge, or a crew and a corner — or drift between them. Just make it believable: reputations are earned over time, not claimed.

Roleplay tips

  • Talk to people. Downtime conversations build the city more than shootouts do.
  • Play to lose sometimes. A scene where your character gets robbed, arrested, or humbled is still a great scene.
  • Never break character to argue. Take issues to a ticket after.
  • If something's confusing, ask in Discord — nobody expects you to know everything.

Get help

Stuck, bugged, or unsure? Open a ticket in Discord and staff will sort it. See Support for how tickets work.
