SAVANNAH STORE

The store isn't open yet. When it is, it runs through Tebex and stays strictly cosmetic and convenience — nothing you buy gives money, weapons, stats, or any edge in the city.

No pay-to-win. Full stop.

Savannah RP follows Rockstar and Tebex monetization rules. Purchases only ever cover cosmetics, personalization, and small convenience — and every product will clearly state exactly what you get.

What the store will offer

  • Supporter tiers (a role, a name colour, a thank-you)
  • Queue priority
  • Custom clothing & character style (cosmetic)
  • Custom chains & jewelry (cosmetic, protected)
  • Vehicle liveries, wraps & vanity plates (cosmetic)
  • Home & business decor / branding (cosmetic)
  • Emote & animation packs

What we will never sell

  • In-game money or currency
  • Weapons, ammo, armor or combat items
  • Stat boosts or gameplay advantages
  • Paid progression or XP
  • Loot boxes / mystery crates / any chance mechanic
  • Staff powers or in-character authority
  • Anything that beats another player for cash

Custom cosmetic orders

Custom chains, clothing and vehicle cosmetics are made to order. When the store opens the flow will be: buy the package on Tebex → open the matching ticket in Discord → provide your Tebex invoice → upload your reference → we build it. If it comes back wrong, the same ticket stays open until support makes it right. Paid custom chains are a protected cosmetic entitlement — they can't be stolen, dropped, transferred, or permanently lost.

By submitting artwork you confirm you own it or have permission to use it. Savannah RP may reject designs containing copyrighted, trademarked, or third-party material.
