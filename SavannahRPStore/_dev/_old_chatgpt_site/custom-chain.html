CUSTOM COSMETIC JEWELRY

CUSTOM CHAIN

Purchase your custom chain first, verify the purchase in a Savannah RP support ticket, then upload the picture you want used.

01

PAY FIRST

Purchase the Custom Chain package through the official Savannah RP Tebex store.

STORE COMING SOON
02

OPEN A TICKET

After payment, open the Custom Chain / Purchase Support ticket in the Savannah RP Discord.

03

VERIFY PURCHASE

Send your Tebex invoice/receipt or purchase transaction/order ID so staff can confirm the payment belongs to you.

04

UPLOAD YOUR PICTURE

After the purchase is verified, upload the exact image or design you want used for the chain directly inside the ticket.

05

WE MAKE IT

Savannah RP reviews the picture and creates the cosmetic chain from the approved design.

06

NEED A FIX?

If the finished chain comes back wrong or does not look right, keep the same ticket open and support will help resolve it.

PURCHASE VERIFICATION

WHAT TO SEND IN YOUR TICKET.

TEBEX RECEIPT / INVOICE

Send the purchase receipt or invoice you received after checkout.

TRANSACTION / ORDER ID

If available, include the Tebex transaction or order identifier shown on the receipt.

PURCHASE EMAIL

Staff may ask for the email used at checkout to match the purchase record. Do not send your password or payment-card details.

VIEW PURCHASE VERIFICATION GUIDE

Savannah RP staff will never need your Tebex password, full debit/credit card number, CVV, or online banking login.

CUSTOM CHAIN POLICY

WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW.

PURCHASE MUST BE VERIFIED

Work does not begin until staff can verify the Custom Chain purchase.

PICTURE REQUIRED

After verification, the buyer uploads the exact picture directly in the support ticket.

YOUR DESIGN

You must own the image or have permission to use it. Unauthorized copyrighted or trademarked material may be rejected.

COSMETIC ONLY

Custom chains do not give money, stats, armor, weapons, inventory space, or any gameplay advantage.

PROTECTED ITEM

Paid custom chains cannot be stolen, dropped, transferred, sold, or permanently lost through normal roleplay.

SUPPORT AFTER DELIVERY

If there is a problem with the finished chain, the same ticket stays open until support resolves it.
