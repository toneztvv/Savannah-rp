FIRST TIME IN SAVANNAH?

NEW PLAYER GUIDE

A simple walkthrough for getting established in Savannah RP without spoiling the city. Learn the basics, build your character, and choose your own path.

01

CREATE YOUR CHARACTER

Build a believable identity with a name, appearance, personality and story. Your character does not need a huge backstory — just enough to know who they are and what they want.

02

LEARN THE BASICS

Read the rules, understand in-character vs out-of-character communication, and learn the basic expectations for semi-serious roleplay.

READ THE RULES →
03

GET ESTABLISHED

Find transportation, basic work, food, clothing and whatever your character needs to start building a life in the city.

04

FIND YOUR PATH

Work a job, apply for PD/EMS, become a mechanic, build a business, enter real estate, work nightlife, or take a completely different route.

05

FIND A HOME

Explore apartments, houses and custom MLO properties. Housing and real estate are intended to be part of the roleplay, not just a menu.

06

BUILD CONNECTIONS

Talk to people. Make friends, business contacts, rivals and allies. Savannah RP is designed around player interaction instead of playing alone.

CHOOSE YOUR LIFE

LEGAL, SOCIAL OR UNDERGROUND.

LEGAL CAREERS

PD, EMS, mechanics, real estate, businesses, services and other city jobs.

BUSINESS & PROPERTY

Open or work at a business, build a brand, buy or rent property, and create jobs for other players.

SOCIAL & LIFESTYLE

Nightlife, events, tattoos, fashion, vehicles, relationships, housing and character-driven stories.

UNDERGROUND

Street networks, criminal opportunities and illegal systems exist, but the city expects players to discover them through roleplay.

NEW PLAYER TIPS

DON'T RUSH THE CITY.

Talk FirstMost of the best RP starts with a conversation, not a weapon.
Ask In CharacterLet your character learn things naturally instead of using outside information.
Take LossesNot every scene needs to be won. Losses often create better stories.
Build SlowlyYou do not need to become rich, powerful or connected on day one.
Use the DirectoryFind businesses, services, housing and departments as Savannah grows.
Report BugsIf something breaks, report it instead of abusing it.
