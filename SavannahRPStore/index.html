A Living City

ONE CITY. EVERY PATH.

Savannah RP is built as a full, living Los Santos — not just a crime server. Legal and illegal life carry equal weight, and every character has room to matter.

Law & Order

PD & EMS

Structured police and emergency services with real dispatch, ranks, and consequences. Serve the city or answer its emergencies.

Make a Living

Careers & Businesses

Mechanics, tattoo artists, nightlife staff, shop owners and player-run businesses. Clock in, build a name, or own the place.

The Streets

Crews & Underground

Gangs, drugs, guns, robberies and territory. A grounded street economy where reputation is earned and lost.

Put Down Roots

Real Estate & Housing

Apartments, houses and custom interiors. Rent a room, buy a home, or set up shop in a custom-built location.

Be Someone

Style & Identity

Custom clothing, tattoos and jewelry that actually show on your character. Look the part of whoever you're playing.

Move Different

Vehicles

A wide vehicle roster with dealerships, garages, finance and impound. Buy it, mod it, and keep it running.

After Dark

Nightlife & Social RP

Clubs, events, relationships and the slower moments. The city keeps going long after the sirens stop.

It Counts

Player-Driven Economy

Legal and illegal money paths that feed the same city. What players do shapes what the city becomes.

Grounded, Not Strict

Semi-Serious RP

Enough structure to keep roleplay meaningful, without turning every scene into a rulebook. Play it real, keep it fun.
