FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU OPEN A TICKET.

Is Savannah RP pay-to-win?

No. Paid packages are intended to focus on cosmetics, community recognition and limited convenience rather than money, weapons, stats or competitive advantages.

Can I buy in-game money?

No. Savannah RP does not sell in-game virtual currency for real money.

Can I buy weapons?

No paid package is intended to give a player combat power or a weapon advantage over other players.

How do custom chains work?

A custom chain is a cosmetic-only entitlement created from approved artwork. Paid custom chains cannot be stolen, dropped, traded, transferred or permanently lost through normal RP.

Can I submit my own image for a chain?

Yes, if you own the artwork or have permission to use it. Savannah RP may reject copyrighted, trademarked, branded or third-party material that is not properly authorized.

What happens if my custom item does not appear?

Open a support request with your Tebex transaction ID and character information. Staff can verify the entitlement and investigate delivery.

Can I transfer a paid cosmetic to another player?

Unless a package specifically says otherwise, paid cosmetic entitlements stay with the original purchaser/eligible character and are not transferable.

Can I get a refund because I was banned?

A ban or other enforcement action does not automatically create a refund right. Refund requests are handled according to the posted Refund Policy and applicable consumer law.

Do purchases guarantee permanent server access?

No. Buying a package does not exempt a player from Savannah RP rules or guarantee access if the player is suspended, banned, or otherwise ineligible to join.

Where do I report a bug or exploit?

Use the official support/report channel. Do not repeatedly reproduce a serious exploit to demonstrate it.
